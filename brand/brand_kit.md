# Casa Mare Crafts Brand Kit

**Brand name:** Casa Mare Crafts  
**Tagline:** Handmade coastal pieces for warm, lived-in beach homes.  
**Style:** Natural, warm, premium, not cartoon beachy.  
**Vibe:** Less souvenir shop, more boutique coastal home brand.

---

## Brand Concept

**Casa Mare Crafts** is a boutique coastal home brand offering handmade pieces inspired by slow living, natural textures, and warm beach homes.

The brand should feel like walking into a beautifully styled coastal house: linen curtains, driftwood, handmade ceramics, woven baskets, sea breeze, neutral tones, and carefully chosen details.

This is **not** a seashell souvenir brand. It is not cartoon beachy, tropical, or loud. It is refined, relaxed, and personal.

---

## Brand Positioning

**For people who want their home to feel like a calm coastal escape, Casa Mare Crafts offers handmade decor pieces that bring warmth, texture, and soul into beach-inspired interiors.**

The key emotional promise:

> **Make your home feel sun-warmed, relaxed, and beautifully lived-in.**

## Typography

### Logo Font Direction

Use an elegant serif with soft curves and premium spacing.

Recommended font options:

- **Cormorant Garamond** — Elegant, artisan, boutique.
- **Canela** — Premium and editorial, if available.
- **Playfair Display** — Accessible, classic, refined.
- **Libre Baskerville** — Warm and trustworthy.

**Best free option:** Cormorant Garamond.

### Website Font Direction

Use a clean sans-serif that feels modern but not sterile.

Recommended font options:

- **Inter** — Clean, readable, modern.
- **Avenir** — Premium and soft.
- **Montserrat** — Clean but slightly more commercial.
- **Lato** — Friendly and approachable.

**Best free option:** Inter.

### Font Pairing Recommendation

- **Logo / Headings:** Cormorant Garamond
- **Website Body:** Inter

This pairing gives the brand a boutique coastal feeling without looking too trendy or fake-premium.

## Logo Direction

The logo should be simple and elegant.

Recommended logo structures:

```text
CASA MARE
CRAFTS
```

Or:

```text
Casa Mare Crafts
```

Use elegant serif typography with wide letter spacing.

### Optional Symbol Ideas

- Minimal arch
- Simple wave line
- Small sun mark
- Subtle hand-drawn shell silhouette
- Roofline + wave combination
- Ceramic vase shape with a wave inside

Be careful here: do not overdo the beach symbol. The second you add a cartoon wave or shell, it starts to look like a souvenir shop. Keep it almost architectural.

## Packaging Direction

Packaging should feel minimal, natural, and premium.

Recommended packaging elements:

- Kraft boxes
- Washed white tissue paper
- Sand beige thank-you cards
- Navy logo stamp
- Cotton string or jute twine
- Small sea glass green detail
- Textured paper labels
- Handwritten note style

Avoid glossy packaging. Matte and textured materials will fit much better.

### Thank You Card Copy

Each piece is made to add warmth, texture, and a little coastal calm to your space.

Casa Mare Crafts

## Brand Rules

### Do

- Use mostly neutral colors, with navy for contrast.
- Keep layouts spacious.
- Use large product photos.
- Use serif typography for elegance.
- Let texture do the work.

### Do Not

- Do not use cartoon beach icons.
- Do not use bright turquoise as the main color.
- Do not make it look like a souvenir shop.
- Do not overuse shells, starfish, anchors, or palm trees.
- Do not use loud tropical patterns.

## Best Overall Brand Direction

The strongest version of Casa Mare Crafts is:

> **A premium handmade coastal home brand with Mediterranean warmth, natural textures, and understated beach-house elegance.**

Do not position it as:

> Crafts for beach lovers.

That sounds cheaper and more generic.

Position it as:

> **Handmade coastal decor for people creating a warm, beautiful beach-inspired home.**
